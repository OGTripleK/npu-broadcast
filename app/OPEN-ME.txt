NPU Broadcast for Windows x64

Extract the whole ZIP before opening NpuBroadcast.exe. Keep the extracted
files together. Use a Windows 11 PC with an AMD Ryzen AI NPU and
an available AMD VitisAI execution provider. The first launch of each
effect may take longer while the NPU compiles its models.

Open NPU Broadcast and click Use existing VB-CABLE. If multiple cables
are installed, choose the one you want and click Name selected cable.
Approve the Windows prompt. If the rename fails, use Settings > System >
Sound > Input to rename that cable's recording endpoint. If your existing
cable is in use, install an extra from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/. The
cable stays visible after the app exits.

Choose Microphone (NPU Broadcast) in your calling or recording app.
NPU Broadcast follows your Windows default input until you select a physical
microphone. With Noise removal and Room echo removal both off, your original
voice passes through.

Audio levels and Diagnostics are in the Voice effects card. Check Listen to
my processed voice to monitor through the Windows default playback device.
Capture starts when either effect is on or another app uses the virtual mic.
In Settings, check Keep microphone active even with effects off to keep the
meters live when no app is using the virtual mic. NPU Broadcast remembers
your settings.

Minimize to keep NPU Broadcast in the notification area. Click its icon or
launch it again to restore the window. Close the window to exit.
